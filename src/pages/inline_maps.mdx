# Inline maps

This page is `src/pages/inline_maps.mdx`. It is written in
[MDX](https://mdxjs.com/): Markdown for the text, plus components such as
`<Map>` wherever you want a map. Each map's layers, colours and popups live in
a YAML file in `src/maps/`.

## Points from a data file, with a popup and a legend

Every sighting from the 2018 Central Park Squirrel Census, coloured by fur
colour. **Click a squirrel** to see its details. The data is
`public/data/squirrel20241021.geojson`; the map is `src/maps/squirrels.yaml`.

<Map config={squirrels} height="60vh" class="map-frame not-prose" />

## Lines from a web address

Manhattan's bike lanes, loaded straight from
[NYC Open Data](https://opendata.cityofnewyork.us/) each time the page opens
(`src/maps/bike-lanes.yaml`). Click a lane for its street name.

<Map config={bikeLanes} height="50vh" class="map-frame not-prose" />

## Turning layers on and off

Give a layer a `label:` and it gets a checkbox in the panel at the top right.
The bike lanes start switched off (`visible: false`); tick them
(`src/maps/layers.yaml`).

<Map config={layerToggles} height="50vh" class="map-frame not-prose" />

## Images

To show an image, put the file in `src/assets/img/`, import it at the top of
the page (`import fiskMap from "../assets/img/fisk02.jpg"`) and show it with
`<Image>`. Astro resizes it to `width` when the site builds: this scan is 4 MB
on disk and much smaller on the page.

<Image
  src={fiskMap}
  width={600}
  alt="Harold Fisk's 1944 map of the ancient courses of the Mississippi River"
/>

A plain Markdown image, `![description](../assets/img/fisk02.jpg)`, works too,
but keeps the image at its full size.

## Embeds

HTML works too, for example an `<iframe>` with a video or another site's map:

<iframe
  title="OpenStreetMap: Central Park"
  src="https://www.openstreetmap.org/export/embed.html?bbox=-73.9819,40.7644,-73.9496,40.8006&layer=mapnik"
  style="width: 100%; height: 300px; border: 0;"
  loading="lazy"
></iframe>
